# Attribution & Use Notice

## Source

Recall-based NEET-PG questions in the `2019/` through `2025/` folders were
compiled from public webpages on PrepLadder, specifically:

- <https://www.prepladder.com/neet-pg-study-material/previous-years-question-papers/neet-pg-previous-year-question-papers-of-last-5-years>

These are not official National Board of Examinations (NBE) papers. NBE does
not publicly release NEET-PG question papers. The compiled questions are
*recall-based* — reconstructed from memory by candidates after each exam — and
the wording, options, and explanations may differ from the original paper.

## Purpose

This collection exists as **personal study notes** for the repository owner's
preparation for NEET-PG 2026. Files have been:

- Reformatted (collapsible-answer Markdown) for self-testing
- Reorganised by subject within each year
- Annotated with brief mechanism/high-yield notes

## Not for Redistribution

The original compilation is owned by PrepLadder. If you are not the repository
owner and intend to reuse this material, please refer to PrepLadder's site
directly and respect their terms of service. The reformatted notes here are
not a substitute for an authorised question bank.

the recall set as a study prompt rather than a definitive answer key and
cross-verify with standard textbooks before relying on any specific fact.
