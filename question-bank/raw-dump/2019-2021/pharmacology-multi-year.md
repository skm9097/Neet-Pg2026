## Batch A — DigiNerve recall-based MCQs (diginerve.com)

Q: INH causes peripheral neuropathy by interfering with which vitamin?
A: Pyridoxine (B6)

Q: Which drug most commonly causes Torsades de Pointes?
A: Sotalol

Q: Best antiemetic for delayed chemotherapy-induced nausea?
A: Aprepitant

Q: Mechanism of action of ivermectin?
A: Enhances GABA-mediated Cl⁻ influx in invertebrates

Q: Which insulin has the longest duration of action?
A: Glargine

Q: Which antihypertensive is contraindicated in pregnancy?
A: Enalapril (ACE inhibitor)

Q: Which antibiotic is unsafe in pregnancy?
A: Tetracycline

Q: First-line topical drug for primary open-angle glaucoma?
A: Timolol

Q: How does rifampicin affect warfarin levels?
A: Decreases INR (rifampicin induces CYP enzymes)

Q: Which is a selective COX-2 inhibitor?
A: Celecoxib

## Batch B — PrepLadder last-5-year PYQ pharmacology (prepladder.com)

Q: Which antineoplastic agent suppresses DNA synthesis?
A: 6-mercaptopurine

Q: How do thiazides increase lithium levels?
A: Thiazides increase tubular reabsorption of lithium

Q: Theophylline is contraindicated with which antibiotic?
A: Erythromycin (CYP3A4 inhibitor → raises theophylline)

Q: Which drug accumulates in the cornea?
A: Chloroquine

Q: Theophylline's diuretic mechanism is via which receptor?
A: Adenosine A1 receptor antagonism

Q: Which drug does NOT cause pulmonary fibrosis?
A: Metformin (methotrexate, bleomycin, nitrofurantoin, methysergide do)

Q: High volume of distribution implies drug is?
A: Sequestered in body tissues

Q: Which antiglaucoma drug is unsafe in infants?
A: Brimonidine (alpha-2 agonist causes CNS depression in neonates)

Q: Phase 2 clinical trial focuses on?
A: Efficacy and dose-finding in small patient groups

Q: Drug of choice for diphtheria carriers?
A: Erythromycin

Q: Drug for opioid withdrawal maintenance therapy?
A: Buprenorphine

Q: Antihypertensive associated with rebound hypertension on withdrawal?
A: Clonidine

Q: SNRI antidepressant (not SSRI)?
A: Reboxetine (selective NRI)

Q: Drug for HIV-induced lipodystrophy?
A: Tesamorelin (growth hormone-releasing hormone analogue)

## Batch C — PrepLadder important questions for NEET-PG 2026 (prepladder.com)

Q: Drug that inhibits uric acid reabsorption in proximal tubules?
A: Probenecid

Q: Drug of choice for infantile spasms with tuberous sclerosis?
A: Vigabatrin

Q: Which drug does NOT cause coronary steal?
A: Nitroglycerin

Q: Clopidogrel targets which receptor?
A: P2Y12 receptor

Q: Mechanism by which insulin lowers potassium?
A: Stimulates Na-K-ATPase → K uptake into cells

Q: CYP2C9 substrate?
A: Phenytoin (CYP1A2-Clozapine, CYP2C19-Pantoprazole, CYP2D6-Codeine, CYP3A4-Midazolam)
