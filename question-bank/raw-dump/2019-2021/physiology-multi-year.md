## Batch A — PrepLadder last-5-years PYQ physiology (prepladder.com)

Q: Management NOT used in altitude sickness?
A: IV digoxin (descent, oxygen, acetazolamide, nifedipine, dexamethasone are used)

Q: Prolactin level is highest when?
A: 24 hours after delivery

Q: JVP "c" wave represents?
A: Tricuspid bulge into right atrium during isovolumetric ventricular contraction

Q: Which protein in GBM provides charge-selective filtration?
A: Heparan sulfate (negatively charged GAG)

Q: Warfarin therapy decreases gamma-carboxyglutamate in which factor?
A: Factor II (prothrombin)

Q: 70 kg male with Na 120 mEq/L — total sodium deficit?
A: 840 mEq (TBW=42L × 20 mEq deficit)

Q: Saturday night palsy — paresis without numbness — which fibres affected?
A: A fibres more susceptible to pressure than C fibres

Q: Baroreceptor reflex is an example of?
A: Negative feedback

Q: Cells signalling neighbouring cells via local diffusion?
A: Paracrine signalling

Q: Cystometrogram segment reflecting Laplace's law?
A: Segment Ib

Q: Resetting of hypothalamic thermostat to fever state — what does NOT occur?
A: Sweating (occurs when fever breaks, not during rise)

Q: In myogenic autoregulation, afferent arteriole contracts in response to?
A: Calcium (stretch-activated Ca²⁺ channels)

Q: NOT a feature of decerebrate rigidity?
A: Rigidity in all body muscles (only antigravity/extensor muscles affected)

Q: Decorticate posturing is characterized by?
A: Upper limb flexion with lower limb extension

Q: RBC preservation in ACD vs CPDA-1?
A: 21 days (ACD) vs 35 days (CPDA-1)

Q: FRC is equal to?
A: ERV + RV (expiratory reserve volume + residual volume)
