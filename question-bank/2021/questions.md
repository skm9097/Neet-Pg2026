## Microbiology

### Q1 — Visceral leishmaniasis vector

A patient from UP with fever, pallor, hepatosplenomegaly. Vector?

- A. Tsetse fly
- B. Triatomine bug
- C. Sandfly
- D. Female anopheles

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C. Sandfly (*Phlebotomus argentipes*)** — Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis) in Bihar, UP, WB.
</details>

### Q2 — Measles virus structure

Unimmunised 2-year-old with coryza, conjunctivitis, bluish-white spots (Koplik). Virus type?

- A. Enveloped ssRNA
- B. Naked ssRNA
- C. Naked dsRNA
- D. Enveloped dsRNA

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A. Enveloped ssRNA** — Measles is a Paramyxovirus, negative-sense ssRNA, enveloped.
</details>

### Q4 — Radiosensitive tissue (NOT)

- A. Spermatogonia
- B. Intestinal epithelium
- C. Neurons
- D. Bone marrow

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

## Physiology

### Q25 — Sodium deficit calculation

30-year-old, 70 kg, sodium 120 mEq/L. Calculate deficit (target 140).

- A. 280 mEq
- B. 480 mEq
- C. 840 mEq
- D. 1400 mEq

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C. 840 mEq** — Deficit = TBW × (target − actual) = (0.6 × 70) × (140 − 120) = 42 × 20 = 840 mEq.
</details>
