# NEET-PG 2026 — Study Plan

A practical schedule for using this question bank. Adjust dates based on your start point and existing prep level.

## Quick stats

- **Exam:** NEET-PG, 200 questions, 3.5 hours, +4/−1 marking
- **Target date:** January 2026 (~7 months from now)
- **In this repo:** 13,665+ questions across all 19 NBE subjects
  - 6,277 year-wise (2015–2025, all 11 years fully covered)
  - 7,388 subject-wise (19 subject pools, year-wise merged in)
- **Concept books:** OBG (65 k words, 212 pages PDF) + PSM (38 pages PDF) in `notes/`

## Phase 1 — Subject foundation (8–10 weeks)

Work through one subject at a time using `subject-wise/*.md`. Target: 2–3 subjects per week (depending on weightage).

| Week | Subjects | Repo files |
|------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Pathology + Anatomy | `pathology.md`, `anatomy.md` |
| 2 | Microbiology + Biochemistry | `microbiology.md`, `biochemistry.md` |
| 3 | Pharmacology + Physiology | `pharmacology.md`, `physiology.md` |
| 4 | Medicine + Surgery | `medicine.md`, `surgery.md` |
| 5 | OBG + Paediatrics | `obstetrics-gynaecology.md`, `pediatrics.md` |
| 6 | Community Medicine + Forensic | `community-medicine.md`, `forensic-medicine.md` |
| 7 | ENT + Ophthalmology + Dermatology | `ent.md`, `ophthalmology.md`, `dermatology.md` |
| 8 | Orthopaedics + Radiology + Anaesthesia + Psychiatry | `orthopaedics.md`, `radiology.md`, `anaesthesia.md`, `psychiatry.md` |

**For each subject:**
1. Read all questions WITHOUT clicking "Answer" — attempt mentally.
2. Mark wrong/uncertain ones (e.g., `[ ] Wrong: Q3, Q7`).
3. Click "Answer" → read mechanism → make brief note.
4. Re-attempt wrong ones at end of week.

## Phase 2 — Year-wise mock testing (4 weeks)

Now switch to `20XX/questions.md` files to simulate the exam:

| Week | Paper |
|------|-------|
| 9 | 2025 (200 Qs — full mock) |
| 10 | 2024 + 2023 + 2022 (combined ~104 Qs) |
| 11 | 2021 + 2020 + 2019 (combined ~94 Qs) |
| 12 | Mixed revision — re-attempt all previously wrong questions |

**Test discipline:**
- Time yourself: 2025 paper in 3 hours 30 minutes.
- Don't peek at answers during the test.
- After the test, calculate your raw score: (correct × 4) − (incorrect × 1).
- Target: 60% + (~480/800) for a competitive rank.

## Phase 3 — Weak topic deep dive (3–4 weeks)

By now you know your weak subjects. For each weak subject:
1. Reopen the subject-wise file.
2. Read every "Answer" mechanism carefully.
3. Cross-reference with a standard textbook (Robbins/Harrison/Goljan/Kaplan).
4. Make a "high-yield facts" page of your own.

## Phase 4 — Final revision (2 weeks)

- Go through all 200 questions in `2025/questions.md` (recall paper).
- Review only the `Answer` blocks (they're written as one-line mechanism notes — perfect for last-minute review).
- Re-do questions you got wrong in Phase 2.

## Phase 5 — Exam week

- Day −7 to −1: NO new content. Only re-read collapsed `<details>` answers.
- Day −1: Mental rest. Light revision of high-yield facts only.
- Day 0: Stay hydrated, sleep 7+ hours the night before.

## How to track progress

In each `questions.md`, add a checkbox next to questions you've mastered:
```markdown
### Q1 ✓ — Topic label
```
Or maintain a separate `progress.md` per subject. Spaced repetition (revisit every 7–14 days) works better than cramming.

---

**Bottom line:** Volume without retention is useless. ~700 well-understood questions beats 5,000 questions skimmed once. Use the collapsible `<details>` blocks for daily active recall — that's the highest-yield study technique for MCQ-based exams.
