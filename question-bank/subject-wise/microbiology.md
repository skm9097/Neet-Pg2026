### Q1 — Oral candidiasis: HIV patient

35-year-old HIV-positive patient with mucosal lesions; microscopy shows budding yeasts and pseudohyphae. Diagnosis?

- A. Oral candidiasis
- B. Hairy leukoplakia
- C. Lichen planus
- D. Diphtheria

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A. Oral candidiasis** — Candida albicans: budding yeasts + pseudohyphae on KOH/Gram stain. Most common oral opportunistic infection in HIV. Hairy leukoplakia (EBV on lateral tongue) does not show pseudohyphae.
</details>

### Q19 — EHEC O157:H7 — "H" antigen

In E. coli O157:H7, "H" represents:

- A. Capsule
- B. Flagella (H antigen = flagellar antigen)
- C. Fimbriae
- D. Lipopolysaccharide

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B. Flagella** — Kauffmann-White scheme: O = somatic LPS antigen; H = flagellar antigen; K = capsular antigen. O157:H7 = somatic serotype 157, flagellar serotype 7. EHEC O157:H7 produces Shiga toxin → HUS.
</details>
