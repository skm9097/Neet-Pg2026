### Q1 — High altitude pulmonary oedema: NOT used

Headache and breathlessness at 3000 m altitude. Which is NOT used in management?

- A. IV digoxin
- B. Immediate descent
- C. Oxygen
- D. Acetazolamide

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A. IV digoxin** — Not part of HAPE/AMS management. Immediate descent is most effective. Oxygen, acetazolamide (prevention), nifedipine (pulmonary vasodilator for HAPE), dexamethasone (for HACE), sildenafil are used.
</details>

The "c" wave in JVP represents:

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

### Q4 — GBM charge-dependent filtration

Which protein in glomerular basement membrane provides charge-selective filtration?

- A. Fibronectin
- B. Heparan sulfate
- C. Collagen IV
- D. Entactin

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B. Heparan sulfate** — Negatively charged GAG in GBM repels negatively-charged albumin → prevents proteinuria. Loss of heparan sulfate (minimal change disease) → albuminuria.

### Q6 — Sodium deficit calculation

70 kg male with serum Na 120 mEq/L. Total Na deficit?

- A. 280 mEq
- B. 480 mEq
- C. 840 mEq
- D. 1400 mEq

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C. 840 mEq** — Na deficit = TBW × (target Na − current Na). TBW = 0.6 × 70 = 42 L. Deficit = 42 × (140 − 120) = 840 mEq. Note: replacement must be slow (≤ 8–10 mEq/L per 24 h) to avoid ODS.
</details>

### Q10 — Cystometrogram: Laplace law segment

In a cystometrogram, which segment reflects Laplace's law?

- B. Segment Ib (Laplace law)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

### Q11 — Hypothalamic thermostat reset: changes NOT occurring

Resetting hypothalamic thermostat from A to C (febrile response). Which is NOT triggered?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

### Q12 — Myogenic autoregulation

In myogenic autoregulation, afferent arterioles contract in response to:

- A. NO
- B. Noradrenaline
- C. Calcium
- D. Adenosine

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Decorticate posturing is characterised by:

- B. Lower limb flexion with upper limb extension
- C. Pronounced rigidity

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D. Upper limb flexion with lower limb extension** — Decorticate: flexion of arms, fists clenched, legs extended. Lesion above red nucleus (cerebrum). Decerebrate (all extension): lesion below red nucleus (brainstem). Decerebrate is worse prognosis.

### Q16 — Functional residual capacity

FRC is:

- A. Volume expired after normal inspiration
- B. Volume after forced expiration
- C. ERV + RV (expiratory reserve + residual volume)
- D. TV + IRV

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

</details>
