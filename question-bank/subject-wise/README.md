## Format

Each question follows the standard schema:
- Short topic label in heading
- Clinical stem or direct question
- 4 options (A–D)
- Collapsible answer with mechanism/explanation

## Coverage

All subject files now include:
1. The original hand-curated high-yield questions (PrepLadder, DigiNerve, firstranker.com)
2. The complete year-wise pool 2015–2025 merged in (under `## Year YYYY` and `## Subject` sections)
3. OBG staging expansions from EduRev, MCQ platforms, and DocPlexus
4. Zero within-file duplicates (stem-key deduplicated)
