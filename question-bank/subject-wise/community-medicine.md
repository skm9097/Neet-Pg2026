### Q4 — Financial advantage quantification in health programmes

Which analysis measures programme outcomes in monetary terms?

- A. Cost-effectiveness analysis
- B. Cost-benefit analysis
- C. Network analysis
- D. Input-output analysis

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

### Q17 — Malaria surveillance: annual blood examination rate

### Q18 — Confounding: incorrect statement

Which is an INCORRECT statement about confounding factors?

- A. Can be reduced by matching in study design
- B. Associated with both the exposure and the outcome
- C. Distributed equally in both study and control groups
- D. Associated with the exposure

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C. Distributed equally in both groups** — A confounding variable is unequally distributed between study groups (that is what makes it a confounder and creates bias). If equally distributed, it cannot confound. Confounders are controlled by matching, stratification, or multivariate analysis.
</details>
