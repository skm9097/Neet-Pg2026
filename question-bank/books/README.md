# Concept Books

Teaching books that transform the raw question pools into readable, concept-first study texts. Unlike the question banks (which are for active-recall self-testing), these are meant to be **read** — they take the recurring exam patterns and explain the surgical/clinical principle behind them, so you can solve modified versions of the same question.

| Book | Built from | Chapters | Focus |
|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| [`surgery-concept-book.md`](surgery-concept-book.md) | `subject-wise/surgery.md` (256 Qs, 2015–2025) | 15 | General Surgery, taught by concept cluster with exam-trap analysis, elimination logic, and one-line memory anchors |

## How these differ from the question banks

- **Question banks** (`subject-wise/`, year folders): attempt-then-reveal MCQs for retrieval practice.
- **Concept books** (here): flowing explanations that cluster related questions, expose the recurring trap, and build a mental model — designed for reading and rapid revision, ending each chapter with a compact recap and a master revision sheet.

## Reading method

Attempt the question first (from the bank), then read the concept here. Revise the **Rapid-Revision Master Sheet** (final chapter) the night before the exam; revise the chapters themselves across the weeks before.
