## 2021_Q60 — biochemistry

- **Q:** Elevated phenylalanine but normal phenylalanine hydroxylase enzyme — deficient cofactor?
- **Got wrong:** B) Pyridoxal phosphate
- **Correct:** A) Biopterin (BH4) ✅
- **Key fact:** Elevated phenylalanine with normal phenylalanine hydroxylase enzyme suggests a deficiency in the cofactor required for the enzyme's activity, which is tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4). BH4 is necessary for the hydroxylation of phenylalanine to tyrosine. Its deficiency impairs this conversion, leading to elevated phenylalanine levels. The correct answer is A) Biopterin (BH4), as it is the cofactor for phenylalanine hydroxylase.
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose pyridoxal phosphate, which is a cofactor for different enzymes, not phenylalanine hydroxylase.
