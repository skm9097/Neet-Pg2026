## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Keratan sulfate is the primary glycosaminoglycan responsible for maintaining corneal transparency due to its ability to regulate collagen fibril spacing and maintain the precise lattice structure of the corneal stroma. This specific arrangement allows for the transmission of light and prevents scattering, thus preserving corneal clarity. Keratan sulfate's unique structure and interactions with collagen fibers are crucial for this function.
