- **Q:** Frameshift mutation at position 4 in a 900-nucleotide mRNA — consequence?
- **Got wrong:** A) No biological change
- **Correct:** C) Complete loss of functional protein ✅
- **Key fact:** A frameshift mutation at position 4 in a 900-nucleotide mRNA would lead to a complete change in the reading frame of the genetic code, resulting in the production of a completely different and likely non-functional protein sequence. This is because the genetic code is read in triplets, and a frameshift mutation alters the sequence of these triplets, leading to the incorporation of incorrect amino acids and often premature stop codons. As a result, the protein product is usually completely non-functional.
