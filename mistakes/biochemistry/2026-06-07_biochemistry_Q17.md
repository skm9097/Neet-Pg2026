## Question

Which enzyme deficiency elevates glutamine (and ammonia)?

## Options

- B) α-galactosidase A
- C) Ornithine transcarbamoylase (OTC) ✅
- D) Arginase

## Your Answer

## Correct Answer

C) Ornithine transcarbamoylase (OTC)

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Ornithine transcarbamoylase (OTC) deficiency leads to an accumulation of carbamoyl phosphate, which in turn increases the synthesis of glutamine and ammonia through alternative pathways. OTC plays a crucial role in the urea cycle, converting carbamoyl phosphate and ornithine to citrulline. Its deficiency impairs the urea cycle, resulting in elevated glutamine and ammonia levels. This is particularly relevant in the context of hyperammonemia and related disorders.
