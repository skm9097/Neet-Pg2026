## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Fasting blood glucose in the early morning primarily comes from hepatic glycogenolysis, which is the breakdown of glycogen stored in the liver. This process occurs during overnight fasting when the body's glucose levels are low, and the liver releases glucose into the bloodstream to maintain blood glucose homeostasis. Hepatic gluconeogenesis also contributes to glucose production, but it is not the primary source in the early morning.
