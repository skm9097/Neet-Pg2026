## 2025_Q98 — obstetrics-gynaecology

- **Q:** G2L2, delivered at home, presents with uterine prolapse. Which structure is weak?
- **Got wrong:** A) Round ligament
- **Correct:** C) Cardinal ligament ✅
- **Key fact:** Uterine prolapse occurs when the supportive structures of the uterus, particularly the cardinal ligaments, are weakened. The cardinal ligaments provide crucial support to the uterus and cervix, and their weakness can lead to prolapse. The correct answer is C) Cardinal ligament, as it is the primary structure that provides lateral support to the cervix and uterus.
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose the round ligament, which is not the primary supportive structure for the uterus in this context.
