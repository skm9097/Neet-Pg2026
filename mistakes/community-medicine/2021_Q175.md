## 2021_Q175 — community-medicine

- **Q:** Comparing hemoglobin between two independent groups — appropriate test?
- **Got wrong:** C) ANOVA
- **Correct:** B) Unpaired (independent) t-test ✅
- **Key fact:** The unpaired (independent) t-test is the appropriate test to compare hemoglobin between two independent groups as it assesses the difference in means between two groups, assuming the data follows a normal distribution. This test is used when the samples are independent and the data is quantitative. The null hypothesis is that the means of the two groups are equal, and the alternative hypothesis is that the means are not equal. The unpaired t-test provides a p-value that indicates whether the difference in means is statistically significant.
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose ANOVA, which is used to compare means among three or more groups, not just two. This indicates a misunderstanding of the appropriate statistical test for comparing two independent groups.
