## 2021_Q196 — community-medicine

- **Q:** There is an outbreak of buboes in a community. What is the vector responsible for this condition?
- **Got wrong:** C) Ixodes tick
- **Correct:** A) Xenopsylla cheopis ✅
- **Key fact:** The correct answer is Xenopsylla cheopis, the Oriental rat flea, which is the primary vector responsible for transmitting the bubonic plague bacterium, Yersinia pestis, from rodents to humans. The bacteria are transmitted through the flea's feces, which contain the bacteria, and enter the human body through cuts or scratches in the skin. This leads to the formation of buboes, or swollen lymph nodes, in the affected individual. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose Ixodes tick, which is the vector for diseases such as Lyme disease, not bubonic plague.
