## Question

A patient with tumour lysis syndrome on Pegloticase. Mechanism of action?

## Options

- A) Xanthine oxidase inhibition
- B) URAT-1 receptor inhibition
- C) Oxidises uric acid ✅
- D) Hydrolysis of uric acid

## Your Answer

D) Hydrolysis of uric acid

## Correct Answer

C) Oxidises uric acid

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Pegloticase is a recombinant uricase enzyme that oxidises uric acid to 5-hydroxyisourate, which is then further broken down to allantoin, a highly soluble compound that is easily excreted by the kidneys. This mechanism of action is crucial in treating tumour lysis syndrome, where rapid cell lysis leads to a massive accumulation of uric acid. The correct answer, C) Oxidises uric acid, reflects this process.
