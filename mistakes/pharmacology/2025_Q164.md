## Question

A patient with tumour lysis syndrome on Pegloticase. Mechanism of action?

## Options

- A) Xanthine oxidase inhibition
- B) URAT-1 receptor inhibition
- C) Oxidises uric acid ✅
- D) Hydrolysis of uric acid

## Your Answer

A) Xanthine oxidase inhibition

## Correct Answer

C) Oxidises uric acid

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Pegloticase is a recombinant uricase enzyme that oxidises uric acid to allantoin, which is more soluble and easily excreted by the kidneys. This mechanism is crucial in treating tumour lysis syndrome, where high levels of uric acid can lead to kidney damage. The correct answer, C) Oxidises uric acid, reflects this unique action of Pegloticase.
