# 2023_Q20

## Question

A patient with numbness/tingling in lateral 3 digits, relieved with arms hanging. Condition and test?

## Options

- A) Guyon's canal syndrome, Froment's test
- B) Carpal tunnel syndrome, Froment's test
- C) Guyon's canal syndrome, Durkan's test
- D) Carpal tunnel syndrome, Durkan's test ✅

## Your Answer

C) Guyon's canal syndrome, Durkan's test

## Correct Answer

D) Carpal tunnel syndrome, Durkan's test

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is D) Carpal tunnel syndrome, Durkan's test. Carpal tunnel syndrome is a condition that causes numbness and tingling in the lateral 3 digits due to compression of the median nerve. Durkan's test is used to diagnose carpal tunnel syndrome by applying pressure to the median nerve. The symptoms being relieved with arms hanging is a classic feature of carpal tunnel syndrome, as it reduces pressure on the median nerve. The condition is often associated with activities that involve repetitive wrist flexion or extension, which can exacerbate the compression of the nerve.

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student incorrectly chose Guyon's canal syndrome, which affects the ulnar nerve and causes symptoms in the medial 2 digits, not the lateral 3 digits. This suggests a lack of understanding of the specific nerve distributions and associated syndromes.
