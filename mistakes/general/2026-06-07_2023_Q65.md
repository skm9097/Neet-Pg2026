## Your Answer

C) Estimated average requirement

## Correct Answer

D) Recommended dietary allowance

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) is the average daily dietary nutrient intake level sufficient to meet the nutrient requirements of nearly all (97–98%) healthy individuals in a particular life stage and gender group. It is established by the National Academy of Sciences and is used to guide dietary recommendations. The RDA is based on the Estimated Average Requirement (EAR), but it is set at a higher level to account for individual variability. The RDA is intended to provide a margin of safety to ensure that most individuals meet their nutritional needs. This concept is crucial in nutrition and public health.
