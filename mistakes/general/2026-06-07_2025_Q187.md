Patient with dyspnea and tachycardia 7 days after knee replacement surgery. Confirming investigation for PE?

## Options

## Correct Answer

D) CT pulmonary angiography

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

CT pulmonary angiography is the confirming investigation for pulmonary embolism (PE) as it provides direct visualization of the pulmonary arteries and can accurately diagnose PE. This is especially important in postoperative patients, such as those who have undergone knee replacement surgery, as they are at increased risk of developing PE. The investigation of choice should be able to directly confirm the presence of a clot in the pulmonary arteries. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student's answer, D-dimer, is incorrect because while D-dimer can be used as a screening test for PE, it is not a confirming investigation and can have false positives, especially in postoperative patients.
