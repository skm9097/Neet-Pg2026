# 2023_Q42

## Question

Patient with abdominal pain, jaundice, and portal hypertension. Anastomosis between which of the following veins is seen?

## Options

- A) Left colic vein and middle colic vein
- B) Esophageal veins and left gastric vein ✅
- C) Superior rectal and phrenic veins
- D) Sigmoid and superior rectal veins

## Your Answer

C) Superior rectal and phrenic veins

## Correct Answer

B) Esophageal veins and left gastric vein

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is B) Esophageal veins and left gastric vein, as portal hypertension leads to the formation of portosystemic anastomoses, where the esophageal veins, which are part of the systemic circulation, anastomose with the left gastric vein, a tributary of the portal vein. This anastomosis is responsible for the development of esophageal varices. The patient's symptoms of abdominal pain, jaundice, and portal hypertension are indicative of liver disease, which can lead to this anastomosis. The anastomosis between the esophageal veins and left gastric vein is a common site for variceal bleeding in patients with portal hypertension.

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student's answer, C) Superior rectal and phrenic veins, is incorrect because this anastomosis is not typically associated with the symptoms described, and the phrenic veins do not typically anastomose with the superior rectal veins in the context of portal hypertension.
