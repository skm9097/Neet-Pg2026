# 2023_Q43

## Question

Patient presents with swelling under the left ear lobule (parotid region) and complains of pain. Most likely cause of pain?

## Options

- A) Facial nerve
- B) Greater auricular nerve ✅
- C) Auriculotemporal nerve
- D) Investing layer of cervical fascia

## Your Answer

C) Auriculotemporal nerve

## Correct Answer

B) Greater auricular nerve

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The greater auricular nerve provides sensory innervation to the parotid region and the area around the ear lobule, making it the most likely cause of pain in this scenario. The nerve arises from the cervical plexus and supplies the skin over the parotid gland and the angle of the jaw. The pain in the parotid region can be referred to the ear, making the greater auricular nerve a key player in the sensation of pain in this area. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student incorrectly chose the auriculotemporal nerve, which is also involved in the parotid region but primarily provides sensory innervation to the temporomandibular joint and the auricle.
