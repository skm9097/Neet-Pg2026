## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is Vitamin B because the diet limited to rice and pulses is deficient in Vitamin B, leading to a condition known as beriberi, which causes a scissor gait. Beriberi is a disease caused by a lack of thiamine (Vitamin B1) and can lead to neurological and cardiovascular problems. Supplementation with Vitamin B can prevent this condition. The scissor gait is a characteristic symptom of beriberi, which is why Vitamin B supplementation is the correct answer.
