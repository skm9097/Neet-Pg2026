## Correct Answer

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is non-fusion of the posterior neuropore, which occurs during the formation of the neural tube and leads to spina bifida, a condition where the spinal cord protrudes through an open vertebral defect. This is often associated with a hairy patch on the back. The posterior neuropore closes around 26-28 days of gestation, and its non-fusion results in spinal cord defects. The clinical presentation of a 1-day-old with a spinal cord protruding through an open vertebral defect and a hairy patch on the back is consistent with this defect.
