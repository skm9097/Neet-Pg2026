## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Broca's area, responsible for motor speech, is located in the inferior frontal gyrus of the frontal lobe, specifically in the opercular and triangular parts of the left hemisphere. This area plays a crucial role in speech production, including articulation and phonology. Damage to Broca's area can result in Broca's aphasia, characterized by non-fluent and labored speech. The correct location is essential for understanding the neural basis of speech and language.
