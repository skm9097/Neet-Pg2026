## 2021_Q48 — anatomy

- **Q:** Cancer near the anal verge metastasizes to which lymph nodes?
- **Got wrong:** C) Internal iliac
- **Correct:** A) Superficial inguinal ✅
- **Key fact:** Cancer near the anal verge primarily metastasizes to the superficial inguinal lymph nodes due to the lymphatic drainage of the anal region. The superficial inguinal lymph nodes are the first echelon lymph nodes for the lower part of the anal canal. This is because the lymphatic vessels from the anal canal drain into the superficial inguinal lymph nodes. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose internal iliac lymph nodes, which are involved in the lymphatic drainage of the upper part of the anal canal, but not the primary site for metastasis from cancers near the anal verge.
