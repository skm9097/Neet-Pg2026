## 2025_Q8 — anatomy

- **Q:** Transitional epithelium (urothelium) lines which structure?
- **Got wrong:** A) Esophagus
- **Correct:** B) Ureter ✅
- **Key fact:** Transitional epithelium, also known as urothelium, lines the structures of the urinary system that stretch, such as the ureters, bladder, and renal pelvis. This type of epithelium is specialized to accommodate the expansion and contraction of these structures. The correct answer is the ureter, which is a muscular tube that transports urine from the kidney to the bladder. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose the esophagus, which is lined by stratified squamous epithelium, not transitional epithelium. This mistake indicates a lack of understanding of epithelial lining in different body structures.
