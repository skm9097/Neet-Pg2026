## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The right coronary artery primarily supplies the right atrium, right ventricle, and the diaphragmatic surface of the heart. The anterior interventricular artery, also known as the left anterior descending artery (LAD), is a branch of the left coronary artery, not the right coronary artery. The right coronary artery does give off branches like the posterior interventricular artery (PDA) and the SA nodal artery in most cases, and the AV nodal artery.
