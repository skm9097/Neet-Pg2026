## 2025_Q62 — forensic-medicine

- **Q:** A patient with RTA, normal CT, dies within 3 hours. Autopsy shows retraction balls and petechial hemorrhages in corpus callosum. Diagnosis?
- **Got wrong:** A) Diffuse vascular injury
- **Correct:** B) Diffuse axonal injury ✅
- **Key fact:** Diffuse axonal injury (DAI) is a type of brain injury caused by shearing forces that occur when the brain is rapidly accelerated or decelerated, leading to retraction balls and petechial hemorrhages in the corpus callosum. This type of injury is common in road traffic accidents (RTA) and can result in death within a short period. The presence of retraction balls and petechial hemorrhages in the corpus callosum is characteristic of DAI. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose diffuse vascular injury, which is not supported by the autopsy findings.
