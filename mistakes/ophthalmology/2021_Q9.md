## 2021_Q9 — ophthalmology

- **Q:** A 33-year-old woman with progressive vision loss in right halves of both eyes. Lesion?
- **Got wrong:** B) Right visual cortex
- **Correct:** A) Left optic tract ✅
- **Key fact:** The correct answer is Left optic tract because a lesion here would cause homonymous hemianopia, resulting in progressive vision loss in the right halves of both eyes. This occurs as the left optic tract carries visual information from the right visual field of both eyes. The left optic tract is responsible for transmitting visual information from the right visual field to the left visual cortex. 
- **Why wrong:** The student's answer, Right visual cortex, is incorrect because a lesion in the right visual cortex would cause homonymous hemianopia on the left side, not the right.
