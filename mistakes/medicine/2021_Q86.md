- **Got wrong:** D) Raynaud's phenomenon
- **Correct:** C) Interstitial lung disease ✅
- **Key fact:** The presence of anti-Scl-70 antibodies is highly specific for systemic sclerosis, and interstitial lung disease (ILD) is a common and significant complication of this condition, often leading to progressive pulmonary fibrosis. ILD is a major contributor to the morbidity and mortality associated with systemic sclerosis. Anti-Scl-70 antibodies are a type of autoantibody that targets topoisomerase I and are closely associated with the development of ILD in these patients. The correct association is with interstitial lung disease, not Raynaud's phenomenon, although Raynaud's can also be present in systemic sclerosis.
