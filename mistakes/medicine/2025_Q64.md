## 2025_Q64 — medicine

- **Q:** A 65-year-old with atrial flutter needs long-term rate control with a calcium channel blocker. Most appropriate?
- **Got wrong:** A) Amlodipine
- **Correct:** C) Diltiazem ✅
- **Key fact:** Diltiazem is the most appropriate calcium channel blocker for long-term rate control in atrial flutter due to its ability to slow the ventricular rate by inhibiting the AV node. It has a more significant effect on the heart rate compared to other calcium channel blockers. This is because Diltiazem has a negative chronotropic effect, making it ideal for rate control in atrial flutter. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose Amlodipine, which is primarily used for hypertension and has less effect on heart rate.
