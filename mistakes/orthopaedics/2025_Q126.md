## 2025_Q126 — orthopaedics

- **Q:** Image shows forward slippage of a vertebra on the one below. Diagnosis?
- **Got wrong:** B) Spondylolysis
- **Correct:** A) Spondylolisthesis ✅
- **Key fact:** Spondylolisthesis refers to the forward slippage of a vertebra on the one below, often due to a defect in the pars interarticularis. This condition can be caused by congenital, traumatic, or degenerative factors. The image shows this characteristic slippage, making spondylolisthesis the correct diagnosis. Spondylolisthesis is often graded based on the degree of slippage, ranging from mild to severe. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose spondylolysis, which refers to a defect in the pars interarticularis without slippage of the vertebra.
