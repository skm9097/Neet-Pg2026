## 2021_Q137 — pathology

- **Q:** Elderly male, cervical lymphadenopathy, bleeding tendency, >20% blasts, Auer rods — cytogenetics?
- **Got wrong:** D) t(9;22)
- **Correct:** B) t(15;17) ✅
- **Key fact:** The correct answer is t(15;17) because it is associated with acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL), which is characterized by bleeding tendency, Auer rods, and >20% blasts. APL is a subtype of acute myeloid leukemia (AML) that often presents with cervical lymphadenopathy. The t(15;17) translocation involves the retinoic acid receptor alpha (RAR-alpha) gene on chromosome 17 and the promyelocytic leukemia (PML) gene on chromosome 15. This translocation is specific to APL and is used for diagnostic purposes. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose t(9;22), which is associated with chronic myeloid leukemia (CML), a different type of leukemia.
