## 2021_Q13 — dermatology

- **Q:** A 30-year-old with flaccid easily-ruptured bullae; biopsy shows suprabasal split. Diagnosis?
- **Got wrong:** B) Pemphigus foliaceus
- **Correct:** A) Pemphigus vulgaris ✅
- **Key fact:** Pemphigus vulgaris is characterized by the formation of flaccid, easily-ruptured bullae due to a suprabasal split in the epidermis, resulting from autoantibodies against desmoglein 3. This condition typically presents with oral mucosal lesions and may progress to involve the skin. The suprabasal split is a hallmark of pemphigus vulgaris, distinguishing it from other pemphigus variants. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose pemphigus foliaceus, which is characterized by a subcorneal split, not suprabasal.
