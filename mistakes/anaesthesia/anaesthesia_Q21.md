## anaesthesia_Q21 — anaesthesia

- **Q:** Recovery position is indicated for:
- **Got wrong:** A) Conscious patient with pulse and breathing
- **Correct:** B) Unconscious patient with pulse and breathing ✅
- **Key fact:** The recovery position is used to maintain a patient's airway when they are unconscious but still breathing and have a pulse. This position helps prevent aspiration of vomit or other fluids and ensures the airway remains open. It is not intended for conscious patients, as they can maintain their own airway and breathing. 
- **Why wrong:** The student incorrectly chose a conscious patient, which is not the correct indication for the recovery position. This suggests a misunderstanding of the purpose of the recovery position
