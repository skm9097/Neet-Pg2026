- **Got wrong:** B) Back slaps
- **Correct:** C) Chest compressions ✅
- **Key fact:** A 3-year-old with peanut aspiration who is unresponsive requires immediate intervention to restore circulation and oxygenation, hence chest compressions are the correct answer. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends CPR for unresponsive children with foreign body aspiration.
