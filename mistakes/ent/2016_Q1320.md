- **Q:** Gene associated with the development of Peutz-Jeghers syndrome is
- **Got wrong:** C) KRAS
- **Correct:** A) STK 11 ✅
- **Key fact:** Peutz-Jeghers syndrome is a rare genetic disorder characterized by the development of benign hamartomatous polyps in the gastrointestinal tract and an increased risk of certain cancers. The STK11 gene, also known as LKB1, is a tumor suppressor gene that plays a crucial role in the development of this syndrome. Mutations in the STK11 gene lead to the formation of these polyps and increase the risk of cancer.
