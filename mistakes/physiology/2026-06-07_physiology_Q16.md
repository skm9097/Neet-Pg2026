## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Functional Residual Capacity (FRC) is the volume of air present in the lungs at the end of a passive expiration, which is the sum of the Expiratory Reserve Volume (ERV) and the Residual Volume (RV). This represents the amount of air left in the lungs after a normal, relaxed exhalation. FRC is an important measure of lung function and is used to diagnose respiratory diseases.
