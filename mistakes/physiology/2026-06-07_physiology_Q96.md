# physiology_Q96

## Question

Vascular injury to inferior frontal gyrus causes:

## Options

- A) Motor speech area loss ✅
- B) Wernicke's area loss
- C) Primary auditory area loss
- D) Visual area loss

## Your Answer

B) Wernicke's area loss

## Correct Answer

A) Motor speech area loss

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The inferior frontal gyrus is associated with motor speech production, specifically Broca's area, which is responsible for speech articulation and expression. Vascular injury to this region would lead to motor speech area loss, characterized by non-fluent aphasia. This is in contrast to Wernicke's area, located in the posterior superior temporal gyrus, which is involved in speech comprehension. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student incorrectly identified Wernicke's area, which is involved in speech comprehension, as the area affected by vascular injury to the inferior frontal gyrus.
