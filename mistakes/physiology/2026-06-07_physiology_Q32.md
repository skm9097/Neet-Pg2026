## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Prosopagnosia, also known as face blindness, is a condition where an individual has difficulty identifying and recognizing known faces, despite having normal vision. This occurs due to damage or abnormalities in the fusiform gyrus, a brain region responsible for face perception. Prosopagnosia can be congenital or acquired, often resulting from brain injury or neurological disorders.
