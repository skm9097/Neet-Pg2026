## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Dipalmitoyl phosphatidylcholine (DPPC) is the main surface-tension-lowering component of pulmonary surfactant, reducing surface tension in alveoli and facilitating breathing. It acts by forming a monolayer at the air-liquid interface, decreasing the energy required to expand the lungs during inhalation. This is crucial for maintaining normal lung function and preventing conditions such as respiratory distress syndrome.
