## Question

β2 receptor action (hyperglycemia) mediated by:

## Options

- A) Adenylyl cyclase ✅
- B) Guanylyl cyclase
- C) Phospholipase C
- D) Phospholipase A2

## Your Answer

B) Guanylyl cyclase

## Correct Answer

A) Adenylyl cyclase

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is Adenylyl cyclase because β2 receptors are G protein-coupled receptors that stimulate adenylyl cyclase, increasing intracellular cyclic AMP (cAMP) levels, which in turn activates protein kinase A and leads to hyperglycemia through glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis. This mechanism is crucial for the 'fight or flight' response. β2 receptors are involved in various physiological responses, including hyperglycemia. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student's answer, Guanylyl cyclase, is incorrect because it is primarily involved in the nitric oxide signaling pathway, not in the β2 receptor-mediated hyperglycemic response.
