## Question

True about the nerve action potential:

## Options

- A) Resting state = point A (before threshold)
- B) Point E = more excitable than normal
- C) C–D = Na⁺ channel opening + K⁺ closure
- D) Point B–D = refractory period ✅

## Your Answer

B) Point E = more excitable than normal

## Correct Answer

D) Point B–D = refractory period

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is D) Point B–D = refractory period, as this is the time during which the nerve is unable to generate another action potential. This occurs because the sodium channels are inactivated and the potassium channels are open, making it difficult for the membrane to depolarize again. The refractory period ensures that the action potential proceeds in one direction only. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student's answer, B) Point E = more excitable than normal, is incorrect because point E is actually the state of hyperpolarization where the nerve is less excitable than normal.
