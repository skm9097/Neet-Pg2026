# physiology_Q33

## Question

All are components of the renal countercurrent mechanism EXCEPT:

## Options

- A) Sodium outflow in thick ascending limb
- B) Water outflow in thin descending limb
- C) Sodium outflow in thin ascending limb ✅
- D) Tubular fluid flow from PCT to DCT

## Your Answer

B) Water outflow in thin descending limb

## Correct Answer

C) Sodium outflow in thin ascending limb

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

The correct answer is C) Sodium outflow in thin ascending limb because the thin ascending limb is highly permeable to water and ions, but sodium is reabsorbed in the thick ascending limb, not the thin ascending limb. The renal countercurrent mechanism involves the flow of tubular fluid from the PCT to the DCT and sodium outflow in the thick ascending limb. Water outflow in the thin descending limb is indeed part of the countercurrent mechanism, as water passively follows the osmotic gradient. The countercurrent mechanism helps maintain the osmotic gradient in the medulla, essential for proper urine concentration.
