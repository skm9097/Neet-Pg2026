## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

Functional residual capacity (FRC) is the volume of air present in the lungs at the end of passive expiration, which is the sum of the expiratory reserve volume (ERV) and the residual volume (RV). It represents the amount of air left in the lungs after a normal exhalation. This concept is crucial in understanding lung volumes and capacities.
