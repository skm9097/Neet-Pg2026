# physiology_Q50

## Question

Neurotransmitter for anterograde (descending) relaxation in peristalsis:

## Options

- A) VIP ✅
- B) Acetylcholine
- C) Substance P
- D) Serotonin

## Your Answer

B) Acetylcholine

## Correct Answer

A) VIP

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Polypeptide) is the neurotransmitter responsible for anterograde relaxation in peristalsis, allowing the smooth muscle to relax and accommodate the bolus of food. This relaxation is essential for the proper movement of food through the digestive tract. VIP acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter, causing smooth muscle relaxation and increased blood flow to the gut. 

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student incorrectly chose Acetylcholine, which is actually an excitatory neurotransmitter involved in stimulating muscle contraction, not relaxation.
