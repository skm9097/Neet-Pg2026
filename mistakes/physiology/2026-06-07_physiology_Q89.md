# physiology_Q89

## Question

RTA + severe muscle injury, K⁺ = 5.5 mEq/L. Resting membrane potential:

## Options

- A) No change
- B) More negative (hyperpolarization)
- C) More positive (depolarization) ✅
- D) First more positive then negative

## Your Answer

B) More negative (hyperpolarization)

## Correct Answer

C) More positive (depolarization)

## Key Fact (generated by LLM)

In the context of severe muscle injury, such as that resulting from a road traffic accident (RTA), there is a significant release of potassium ions (K⁺) into the bloodstream, leading to hyperkalemia. This increase in extracellular potassium concentration reduces the resting membrane potential, making it more positive (depolarization), as the resting membrane potential is inversely related to the logarithm of the extracellular potassium concentration. This is a critical concept in understanding the pathophysiology of muscle injury and its effects on membrane potential.

## Why You Got It Wrong (generated by LLM)

The student mistakenly chose hyperpolarization, which is the opposite of what occurs in this scenario, indicating a lack of understanding of the relationship between potassium levels and membrane potential.
